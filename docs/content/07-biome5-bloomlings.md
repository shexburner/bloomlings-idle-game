# Biome 5 Bloomlings — The Scorched Expanse (Zones 101-125)

## Biome Intro

Beyond the Crystal Caverns, the earth exhales heat. The Scorched Expanse is a volcanic plateau where rivers of molten stone carve slow paths through fields of black basalt and ash. The air shimmers, the ground cracks, and nothing here should be alive — but life is stubborn, and the Bloomlings who have taken root in this furnace burn with a defiance that makes the lava look lukewarm. This is post-Transcendence territory: only Gardeners who have been reborn in Starlight reach this place.

### Cinderbloom
**Rarity**: Uncommon
**Biome**: The Scorched Expanse
**Appearance**: A wiry, heat-blackened Bloomling whose petals are curled sheets of cooled magma — dark grey on the outside, glowing ember-orange along the cracks. Tiny sparks drift from its crown like lazy fireflies, and its eyes are bright amber, fierce and unblinking. It leaves faint scorch marks where it stands.
**Personality**: Scrappy, defiant, fiercely optimistic
**Backstory**: Cinderbloom sprouted from a crack in a lava field where no seed should have survived. It didn't just survive — it caught fire and decided it liked the warmth. Every petal is a scar it wears proudly.
**Evolution Path**: Emberseed -> Cinderbloom -> Cinderbloom Infernal
**Ability (Bloom)**: +4% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Floral, Embered
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 105
**Flavor Quote**: "You call this hot? I call this Tuesday."

### Magmaroot
**Rarity**: Rare
**Biome**: The Scorched Expanse
**Appearance**: A hulking, broad-trunked Bloomling whose bark is volcanic rock — cracked and veined with glowing magma that pulses like a heartbeat. Thick roots plunge into the ground and emerge steaming. Its crown is a cluster of heat-resistant fronds that glow red at the tips, and its deep-set eyes smolder like coals in a dying fire.
**Personality**: Patient, immovable, quietly furious
**Backstory**: Magmaroot grew downward first, threading its roots through magma channels until it found the planet's heartbeat. It draws strength from the deep heat and shares it reluctantly, like a furnace that resents being useful.
**Evolution Path**: Lavaling -> Magmaroot -> Magmaroot Titan
**Ability (Bloom)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +16% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Stonekin, Embered
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 112
**Flavor Quote**: "I am rooted in the fire beneath the fire."

### Ashveil
**Rarity**: Epic
**Biome**: The Scorched Expanse
**Appearance**: An ethereal, smoke-wreathed Bloomling whose body is a column of swirling ash and cinder held together by an inner glow of white-hot light. Its petals are sheets of volcanic glass — obsidian-black and razor-thin — that fan out like dark wings. Its eyes are pale white, almost ghostly, and it moves without sound, trailing wisps of warm ash.
**Personality**: Mysterious, melancholic, fiercely protective
**Backstory**: Ashveil is what remains when a forest fire ends and the smoke refuses to disperse. It gathered itself from the ashes of a thousand burned things and chose to become something new — not a memorial, but a promise that what burns can bloom again.
**Evolution Path**: Ashling -> Ashveil -> Ashveil Phoenix
**Ability (Bloom)**: -5% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Ability (Elder)**: -10% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Synergy Tags**: Embered, Luminous
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 118
**Flavor Quote**: "I am the garden that grows after the fire."

### Pyranthus
**Rarity**: Epic
**Biome**: The Scorched Expanse
**Appearance**: A towering, regal Bloomling whose body is a pillar of crystallized flame — frozen mid-eruption, yet radiating intense heat. Its crown is a burst of fire-colored petals — scarlet, gold, and white — that flicker and dance as if alive. Its eyes are molten gold, imperious and ancient, and the ground around it glows faintly orange.
**Personality**: Proud, commanding, secretly compassionate
**Backstory**: The Molten Throne was not a seat of power — it was a prison. Pyranthus was the volcanic vent's guardian, bound to contain the eruption. When the Gardener proved the surface world was worth protecting, Pyranthus released its vigil and rose, free at last.
**Evolution Path**: Pyresprout -> Pyranthus -> Pyranthus Eternal
**Ability (Bloom)**: +12% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +24% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Embered, Radiant
**Unlock Condition**: Defeat the Biome 5 Boss (The Molten Throne) at Zone 125
**Flavor Quote**: "I held the fire so the world could sleep. Now I carry it so the world can grow."

## Biome Boss: The Molten Throne

**Zone**: 125
**Description**: At the heart of the Scorched Expanse, a volcanic vent rises like a throne of liquid fire. The Molten Throne is not a creature — it is a *place* that has become aware, a geological event with opinions. It erupts in slow, deliberate pulses, each one a challenge: *prove you can grow where I burn*.
**Mechanic**: Standard boss fight (HP bar = 50x zone threshold, 2-minute timer, tap value doubled). Every 30 seconds, the Throne erupts, temporarily reducing idle production by 50% for 5 seconds — forcing active tapping during eruption phases.
**Reward**: Pyranthus (Epic Bloomling), unlocks Biome 6 (The Abyssal Depths)
