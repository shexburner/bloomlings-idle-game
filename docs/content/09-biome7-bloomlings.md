# Biome 7 Bloomlings — The Celestial Canopy (Zones 151-175)

## Biome Intro

Above the world, above the clouds, the trees kept growing. The Celestial Canopy is a forest that forgot to stop — its trunks are pillars of living wood that pierce the cloud layer, and its branches spread into a second sky of leaves and light. Up here, the air is thin and sweet, the stars feel close enough to touch, and the Bloomlings drift between branches on currents of warm wind, tethered to nothing but their own stubborn will to bloom. Gravity is a suggestion. Wonder is mandatory.

### Zephyrbud
**Rarity**: Uncommon
**Biome**: The Celestial Canopy
**Appearance**: A small, buoyant Bloomling shaped like a dandelion puff — a sphere of silky, iridescent filaments that catch the wind and keep it perpetually airborne. Its tiny face peeks out from the center of the puff, bright-eyed and windswept, and its laughter sounds like wind chimes in a breeze.
**Personality**: Playful, restless, impossible to pin down
**Backstory**: Zephyrbud was a seed that the wind caught and never put down. It has circled the Canopy a thousand times, and each lap it discovers something new — a branch it missed, a star it hadn't named, a cloud shaped like a Bloomling it once knew.
**Evolution Path**: Breezeling -> Zephyrbud -> Zephyrbud Galeborn
**Ability (Bloom)**: +4% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Floral, Aerial
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 155
**Flavor Quote**: "Catch me if you — oh look, a cloud!"

### Skybloom
**Rarity**: Rare
**Biome**: The Celestial Canopy
**Appearance**: A tall, elegant Bloomling whose stem is a spiral of pale wood that corkscrews upward, crowned with broad, translucent petals that glow with captured starlight. Its leaves are thin and silver-veined, and they angle themselves to catch light from both sun and stars simultaneously. Its eyes are pale gold, calm and far-seeing.
**Personality**: Contemplative, ambitious, quietly joyful
**Backstory**: Skybloom grew on the highest branch of the tallest tree and still wasn't satisfied. It stretched until its petals broke through the last layer of leaves and touched open sky. It was the first Bloomling to see the stars without a canopy between them.
**Evolution Path**: Starsprout -> Skybloom -> Skybloom Astral
**Ability (Bloom)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +16% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Aerial, Radiant
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 162
**Flavor Quote**: "There's always a higher branch."

### Cloudweaver
**Rarity**: Epic
**Biome**: The Celestial Canopy
**Appearance**: A wispy, half-translucent Bloomling whose body is woven from cloud-stuff and morning dew. Its form shifts constantly — sometimes a flower, sometimes a bird, sometimes just a smiling face in a drift of mist. Tiny raindrops orbit it like satellites, and its eyes are the pale blue of a sky just after rain.
**Personality**: Dreamy, creative, gently chaotic
**Backstory**: Cloudweaver was a cloud that drifted too close to the Canopy and got tangled in the branches. Rather than dissipate, it decided to stay — weaving itself a body from mist and memory, becoming the first Bloomling made entirely of weather.
**Evolution Path**: Mistling -> Cloudweaver -> Cloudweaver Stormborn
**Ability (Bloom)**: -5% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Ability (Elder)**: -10% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Synergy Tags**: Aerial, Luminous
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 168
**Flavor Quote**: "I used to be rain. Now I'm something the rain dreams about."

### Aethervane
**Rarity**: Epic
**Biome**: The Celestial Canopy
**Appearance**: A striking, angular Bloomling whose body is a lattice of crystallized wind — visible only as faint refractive distortions in the air, like heat shimmer given form. Its petals are razor-thin sheets of solidified atmosphere that hum at different pitches, and its eyes are twin points of electric blue. It doesn't walk — it *arrives*, carried by currents only it can feel.
**Personality**: Precise, commanding, deeply honorable
**Backstory**: The Stormveil was the Canopy's crown — a perpetual cyclone that kept the highest branches in eternal twilight. Aethervane was its eye, the calm center that held the storm together. When the Gardener's light pierced the Stormveil, Aethervane chose peace over power.
**Evolution Path**: Galeling -> Aethervane -> Aethervane Sovereign
**Ability (Bloom)**: +12% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +24% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Aerial, Thorned
**Unlock Condition**: Defeat the Biome 7 Boss (The Stormveil) at Zone 175
**Flavor Quote**: "I was the storm's heart. Now I am the garden's wind."

## Biome Boss: The Stormveil

**Zone**: 175
**Description**: The crown of the Celestial Canopy is shrouded by the Stormveil — a perpetual cyclone of wind, lightning, and cloud that has raged since before the trees reached this height. It is not malicious; it is *elemental*, a force of nature that simply exists. To pass through it is to prove that your light can burn steady in a gale.
**Mechanic**: Standard boss fight (HP bar = 50x zone threshold, 2-minute timer, tap value doubled). The Stormveil creates "Wind Gusts" every 15 seconds that scatter tap targets across the screen for 3 seconds, requiring faster reflexes. Successfully tapping during gusts grants +50% tap value.
**Reward**: Aethervane (Epic Bloomling), unlocks Biome 8 (The Frozen Thicket)
