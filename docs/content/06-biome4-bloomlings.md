# Biome 4 Bloomlings — The Crystal Caverns (Zones 76-100)

## Biome Intro

Beneath the Twilight Hollow, the earth opens. The Crystal Caverns are a vast underground cathedral of living stone, where crystalline formations refract even the faintest light into prismatic cascades of color. Underground streams murmur through channels of quartz, and the walls themselves seem to breathe — expanding and contracting in slow geological sighs. Nothing here is soft. Everything is beautiful. The Bloomlings who take root in this place have learned to grow where nothing should.

---

### Crystalbloom
**Rarity**: Rare
**Biome**: The Crystal Caverns
**Appearance**: A compact, angular Bloomling whose petals are translucent crystal shards — pale rose and ice-blue — arranged in a geometric rosette. Its stem is a lattice of mineral threads, and its eyes are faceted like cut gemstones, refracting light into tiny rainbows on nearby surfaces. It chimes softly when it moves, like a wind chime made of glass.
**Personality**: Precise, analytical, unexpectedly warm
**Backstory**: Crystalbloom grew from a seed that fell into a mineral spring and refused to dissolve. Instead, it mineralized — replacing cellulose with crystal, chlorophyll with refracted light. It thinks in angles and speaks in harmonics, but its heart is still a seed.
**Evolution Path**: Shardling -> Crystalbloom -> Crystalbloom Archon
**Ability (Bloom)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +16% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Radiant, Stonekin
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 80
**Flavor Quote**: "Every fracture lets more light in."

### Gemsprout
**Rarity**: Epic
**Biome**: The Crystal Caverns
**Appearance**: A small but intensely radiant Bloomling that resembles a cluster of raw gemstones — emerald, sapphire, and amber — fused into a vaguely floral shape. Its body pulses with internal light, and each facet projects a different color onto the cavern walls. Its eyes are deep emerald, and it vibrates faintly with a resonance that other Bloomlings find soothing.
**Personality**: Energetic, generous, slightly overwhelming
**Backstory**: Gemsprout didn't grow — it crystallized, layer by layer, over decades of mineral drip. When the Gardener's light first reached the deep caverns, Gemsprout shattered its outer shell and burst into color like a geode cracked open by wonder.
**Evolution Path**: Gemseed -> Gemsprout -> Gemheart Sovereign
**Ability (Bloom)**: +4% critical tap chance
**Ability (Elder)**: +8% critical tap chance
**Synergy Tags**: Radiant, Luminous
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 88
**Flavor Quote**: "I've been saving this light for you. All of it. Take it!"

### Prismaflora
**Rarity**: Legendary
**Biome**: The Crystal Caverns
**Appearance**: A breathtaking Bloomling of impossible geometry — a flower that seems to exist in more dimensions than three. Its petals are sheets of pure prismatic light, shifting through every color in slow, hypnotic waves. Its stem is a spiral of interlocking crystal rings, and its eyes are white-gold, serene, and fathomless. Looking at Prismaflora too long makes the edges of the world feel soft.
**Personality**: Serene, enigmatic, gently overwhelming
**Backstory**: Prismaflora was not grown. It was *refracted* into existence when the Quartz Colossus shattered and its light passed through a thousand crystal facets at once. It is less a Bloomling and more a living prism — a place where light goes to remember what it could be.
**Evolution Path**: Prismling -> Prismaflora -> Prismaflora Ascendant
**Ability (Bloom)**: +20% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +40% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Radiant, Luminous
**Unlock Condition**: Defeat the Biome 4 Boss (The Quartz Colossus) at Zone 100
**Flavor Quote**: "I am every color the light forgot to be."

**Lore**: Crystalbloom's precise angles and Gemsprout's raw radiance complement each other like a lens and a flame. Together, they turn the cavern into a kaleidoscope — and the Sunlight output into something the economy spreadsheet calls "very healthy."

**Bonus**: +15% Sunlight production for both
**Lore**: Gemsprout's vibrations harmonize with Prismaflora's light-bending nature, creating standing waves of color that pulse through the cavern. Other Bloomlings describe it as "watching a sunrise inside a diamond."

**Pair**: Crystalbloom + Prismaflora
**Bonus**: +10% idle Sunlight production for both

## Biome Boss: The Quartz Colossus

**Zone**: 100
**Description**: The deepest chamber of the Crystal Caverns is sealed by the Quartz Colossus — a towering crystalline golem that has stood motionless for millennia, its body a lattice of interlocking quartz pillars. It does not attack. It simply *endures*, reflecting all light back at its source. To defeat it is not to break it, but to fill it with so much light that it becomes transparent — and the path beyond shines through.
**Mechanic**: Standard boss fight (HP bar = 50x zone threshold, 2-minute timer, tap value doubled). At 25% HP, the Colossus becomes transparent, and all taps generate Resonance echoes (bonus +30% tap value for the remainder of the fight).
**Reward**: Prismaflora (Legendary Bloomling), unlocks Biome 5 (The Scorched Expanse, post-Transcendence)
