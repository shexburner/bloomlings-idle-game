# Biome 1 Bloomlings — The Mossy Cradle (Zones 1-25)

## Biome Intro

The Mossy Cradle is an old greenhouse that the world forgot and the rain remembered. Cracked glass lets in pillars of warm light, and every surface is blanketed in soft green moss, as if the building itself decided to become a garden. It is quiet here, but not empty — small things are stirring, uncurling, opening their eyes for the first time.

---

### Fernley
**Rarity**: Common
**Biome**: The Mossy Cradle
**Appearance**: A small, bright-green fern with tightly curled fronds that uncurl one by one when content. Two tiny dot-eyes blink from the center of its rosette, and its roots trail behind it like a nervous tail, constantly fidgeting.
**Personality**: Curious, gentle, slightly nervous
**Backstory**: The first Bloomling to sprout in the abandoned greenhouse. For weeks it was alone, watching dust motes dance in the light. It learned patience from the silence and kindness from the rain that leaked through the broken roof.
**Evolution Path**: Fiddlehead -> Fernley -> Ancient Fern
**Ability (Bloom)**: +2% idle Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +4% idle Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Mossy, Verdant
**Unlock Condition**: Available at game start (Zone 1). Fernley is the player's first Bloomling.
**Flavor Quote**: "Every new day is a new frond to unfurl."

---

### Mosswick
**Rarity**: Common
**Biome**: The Mossy Cradle
**Appearance**: A round, squat creature covered in dense velvety moss, resembling a mossy stone that grew legs. Its eyes are half-lidded and drowsy, and a thin wisp of spore-dust drifts lazily from the top of its head like slow smoke.
**Personality**: Easygoing, drowsy, quietly dependable
**Backstory**: Mosswick didn't so much sprout as accumulate. One day the moss on the greenhouse floor simply gathered itself up and blinked. It doesn't move fast, doesn't worry much, and produces Sunlight with the steady patience of stone.
**Evolution Path**: Mosslet -> Mosswick -> Mossmonolith
**Ability (Bloom)**: +1 to base tap value
**Ability (Elder)**: +2 to base tap value
**Synergy Tags**: Mossy, Stonekin
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 3
**Flavor Quote**: "Slow's not the same as stopped."

### Petaline
**Rarity**: Uncommon
**Biome**: The Mossy Cradle
**Appearance**: A slender wildflower Bloomling with layered pink-and-white petals that shift color with mood — blushing deeper when praised, fading to pale lavender when thoughtful. Her stem is graceful and slightly swayed, and two bright eyes peer out from the center of her bloom like secrets.
**Personality**: Expressive, vain (in a charming way), theatrical
**Backstory**: Petaline pushed through a crack in the greenhouse tiles and immediately demanded to know where the sun was brightest. She arranges herself to catch every stray beam of light, convinced the world is a stage and she is the opening number.
**Evolution Path**: Petalette -> Petaline -> Petalion
**Ability (Bloom)**: +4% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Ability (Elder)**: +8% Sunlight production to all Garden Bloomlings
**Synergy Tags**: Verdant, Luminous
**Unlock Condition**: Reach Zone 10
**Flavor Quote**: "Stand where the light is. Always."

---

### Thornwick
**Rarity**: Rare
**Biome**: The Mossy Cradle
**Appearance**: A compact, armored Bloomling bristling with dark green thorns and woody bark-plating. Beneath the thorns, clusters of tiny white flowers bloom stubbornly. Its eyes are sharp and watchful, glinting from the shadow of a thorny crest like a knight's visor.
**Personality**: Protective, stoic, secretly tender
**Backstory**: Thornwick grew from the thickest tangle of the Overgrowth — the great vine that once sealed the greenhouse door. When the Gardener cleared the way, Thornwick was the heart left behind, loyal now to the one who set it free.
**Evolution Path**: Thornsprout -> Thornwick -> Thornwarden
**Ability (Bloom)**: -3% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Ability (Elder)**: -6% zone Sunlight threshold for all zones
**Synergy Tags**: Thorned, Mossy
**Unlock Condition**: Defeat the Biome 1 Boss (The Overgrowth) at Zone 25
**Flavor Quote**: "I was the wall. Now I am the gate."

## Synergy Pairs

### Undergrowth Alliance
**Pair**: Fernley + Mosswick
**Bonus**: +15% idle Sunlight production for both
**Lore**: The first two Bloomlings in the greenhouse grew up together in the same patch of damp earth. Fernley's curiosity and Mosswick's calm balance each other — one reaches, the other holds steady.

### Bloom and Briar
**Pair**: Petaline + Thornwick
**Bonus**: +15% Sunlight production for both
**Lore**: Petaline's vanity meets Thornwick's stoicism. She calls him her "stage guard." He calls her "noisy." But nothing gets past his thorns to harm her petals, and nothing brightens his shadow like her glow.

### Cradle Guard
**Pair**: Mosswick + Thornwick
**Bonus**: +10% tap value for both
**Lore**: Stone and thorn, patience and vigilance. Together they are the walls of the Mossy Cradle — nothing enters uninvited, and nothing leaves unnoticed. Mosswick provides the foundation; Thornwick provides the teeth.
