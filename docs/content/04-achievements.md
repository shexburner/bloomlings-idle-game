## Growth Achievements

**First Sprout** — "Every garden begins with a single seed."
Condition: Unlock your first Bloomling
Reward: 200 Sunlight

**Full Bloom** — "Look at you, all grown up and photosynthesizing."
Condition: Evolve any Bloomling to Bloom stage
Reward: 1,000 Sunlight

**Wise Beyond Your Ears** — "Not all Bloomlings have ears, but the Elder ones listen the hardest."
Condition: Evolve any Bloomling to Elder stage
Reward: 5,000 Sunlight + 3 Dewdrops

**The Collector** — "You're not hoarding. You're curating."
Condition: Own 10 unique Bloomlings
Reward: 2,500 Sunlight

**Greenhouse Gala** — "Everyone's invited. Especially the ones with thorns."
Condition: Own 25 unique Bloomlings
Reward: 10,000 Sunlight + 5 Dewdrops

**Best Friends** — "Some roots just fit together."
Condition: Discover your first Bloomling synergy
Reward: 500 Sunlight

**Dream Team** — "Four synergies active at once. The garden is practically humming."
Condition: Have 4 Bloomlings with active synergies in the Garden simultaneously
Reward: 5,000 Sunlight + 5 Dewdrops

**Elder Council** — "When the Elders gather, even the weeds pay attention."
Condition: Have 3 Elder-stage Bloomlings in the Garden at the same time
Reward: 8,000 Sunlight + 5 Dewdrops

## Hidden Achievements

**Patient Gardener** — "Sometimes the best thing a gardener can do is walk away for a while."
Condition: Leave the app closed for exactly 24 hours, then return
Reward: 10 Dewdrops

**Speed Demon** — "Ten zones in under a minute. The Bloomlings got whiplash."
Condition: Clear 10 zones in under 60 seconds
Reward: 8 Dewdrops

**Stubborn Sprout** — "You failed the gate ten times and then headbutted through it on the eleventh. Respect."
Condition: Fail a gate zone 10 times, then beat it
Reward: 5 Dewdrops

**Hat Trick** — "Five hats and counting. Your Bloomlings are the best-dressed creatures in the greenhouse."
Condition: Purchase 5 different Bloomling cosmetic hats
Reward: 5 Dewdrops

**Night Owl** — "The garden grows in the dark, too. You just have to be awake to see it."
Condition: Collect offline earnings five times between midnight and 5 AM
Reward: 8 Dewdrops

**Completionist** — "Every achievement unlocked. You've seen everything the garden has to offer. Or have you?"
Condition: Unlock every other achievement
Reward: 50 Dewdrops
