## Tap Upgrades

**Stronger Rays** — Your fingertip channels a little more sunlight each time, like a magnifying glass held just right.
Effect: +1 base tap value per level

**Focused Light** — Less scatter, more splatter. Every tap delivers a concentrated beam of warm, golden energy.
Effect: +3 base tap value per level

**Lucky Fingers** — Some gardeners just have the touch. Yours occasionally crackles with unexpected brilliance.
Effect: +1% critical tap chance per level (soft cap at +25%, diminishing returns beyond)

**Solar Flare** — A tap so bright your Bloomlings squint. Worth it.
Effect: +5 base tap value per level

**Prismatic Touch** — Each successive tap splits the light further, bending every color into power. The longer you keep going, the wilder it gets.
Effect: +0.01 combo coefficient per level (base 0.05)

**Radiant Burst** — At this point, tapping the screen is basically a controlled detonation of photosynthetic joy.
Effect: +15 base tap value per level

## Idle Upgrades

**Fertile Soil** — Rich, dark, and crumbly. The kind of dirt that makes earthworms write poetry.
Effect: +0.05x idle multiplier per level

**Sunlit Canopy** — The leaves overhead part just enough to let the good light through, bathing the garden in a steady golden glow.
Effect: +0.10x idle multiplier per level

**Root Network** — Beneath the surface, roots whisper secrets of growth to one another. Everyone benefits from the gossip.
Effect: +5% to all Bloomling base production per level

**Growth Spurt** — Sometimes a garden doesn't grow gradually. Sometimes it happens overnight, and the Bloomlings wake up taller and very confused.
Effect: +0.20x idle multiplier per level

**Ancient Wisdom** — The oldest roots remember things the young shoots haven't learned yet, and they're finally willing to share.
Effect: +2% synergy bonus effectiveness per level

**Greenhouse Effect** — The garden has become its own little world now, warm and humming with energy even when no one's watching.
Effect: +0.50x idle multiplier per level

## Nectar Upgrades (Rebirth Shop)

**Enriched Soil** — Last season's Nectar seeped deep into the earth, and this season's dirt practically glows with potential.
Effect: +25% Sunlight production per level (max level 20)

**Stronger Roots** — Each Rebirth tempers the roots like steel in a forge. Every tap now strikes with the memory of every garden before.
Effect: +20% tap value per level (max level 20)

**Rapid Growth** — The soil remembers the shape of every Bloomling it ever held, and they fill that shape a little faster each time.
Effect: Bloomlings level up 10% cheaper per level (max level 15)

**Garden Expansion** — The greenhouse walls crack outward just a little more, making room for one more friend in the dirt.
Effect: +1 Garden slot per level (max level 2)

**Seasonal Memory** — Spring doesn't start from scratch anymore. The garden remembers how far it got, and skips ahead to where it left off.
Effect: Start each Rebirth at zone 5/10/15/20 instead of zone 1 (max level 4)

**Nectar Roots** — Some roots grow so deep they tap into last season's Nectar before it fully drains. Each Rebirth leaves a little more behind.
Effect: +10% Nectar earned on future Rebirths per level (max level 10)

**Bloom Retention** — The Bloomlings hold onto their petals through the turning of the season. They've learned how to weather the change.
Effect: Bloomlings keep Bloom stage through Rebirth (max level 1)

**Elder Retention** — The eldest Bloomlings have seen enough winters. They don't flinch when the season turns. They simply endure.
Effect: Bloomlings keep Elder stage through Rebirth (max level 1)

**Deep Roots** — Even when you're away, the roots keep working, drawing warmth from deep places you can't see.
Effect: +10% offline production rate per level (max level 10)

**Combo Memory** — Your fingers may forget, but the garden doesn't. The rhythm of last season's tapping echoes in the soil.
Effect: Combo meter starts at 10 after Rebirth instead of 0 (max level 1)

## Essence Upgrades (Transcendence Shop)

**Primordial Vigor** — The Essence beneath the garden is older than sunlight. It remembers a time when everything grew without trying.
Effect: All Sunlight production x2 per level (max level 10)

**Ancient Wisdom** — The Essence has been listening for millennia, absorbing the whispered lessons of every garden that ever was.
Effect: All Nectar earnings x1.5 per level (max level 10)

**Eternal Garden** — Some gardens exist outside of time. Yours is becoming one of them, with room for just a little more life.
Effect: +1 permanent Garden slot per level (max level 2)

**Biome Attunement** — The Essence hums in harmony with every biome, unlocking their unique magic from the very first step.
Effect: Unlock biome special mechanics from Zone 1 (max level: per biome)

**Accelerated Seasons** — Seasons that once took weeks now pass in the blink of an eye. The garden has learned to fast-forward through the boring parts.
Effect: Start Rebirths at higher zones per level (max level 5)

**Bloomling Awakening** — Deep in the Essence, something legendary stirs. Shapes that haven't existed since the world was young begin to take form.
Effect: Unlock Legendary-tier Bloomlings (max level 1)

**Essence Conduit** — The roots now reach so deep they tap the Essence directly, pulling up more power each time the cycle completes.
Effect: +15% Essence earned on future Transcendences per level (max level 5)

**Mythic Seedbed** — At the bottom of everything, where Essence pools into something older than memory, the Mythic seeds have been waiting.
Effect: Unlock Mythic-tier Bloomlings (also requires Dewdrops) (max level 1)

**Cosmic Roots** — The roots have grown through the earth and out the other side, drinking starlight from places that don't have names.
Effect: Offline progress rate increased toward 100% per level (from default 50%) (max level 3)
