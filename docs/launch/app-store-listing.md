## Description (App Store & Google Play)

Something stirs in the old greenhouse.

Tiny leaves unfurl. A pair of bright eyes blinks open. The first Bloomling has awakened — and it's been waiting for you.

**Bloomlings** is a cozy idle game about growing magical plant creatures, exploring enchanted biomes, and building the most wondrous garden the world has ever seen. Tap to channel Sunlight, awaken sleeping Bloomlings, and watch your greenhouse come alive.

🌱 TAP, GROW, EVOLVE
Channel Sunlight with every tap. Build combos for massive bursts of energy. Watch your Bloomlings grow from tiny Sprouts into magnificent Elders with unique abilities and personalities.

🌿 COLLECT THEM ALL
Discover 28+ Bloomlings across 8 enchanted biomes — from the misty Mossy Cradle to the shimmering Crystal Caverns. Each Bloomling has a name, a story, and a reason to be in your garden.

🌻 BUILD YOUR DREAM GARDEN
Choose which Bloomlings to nurture in your Garden. Unlock synergies between species — pair the right Bloomlings and watch the magic multiply. Every garden tells a different story.

🔄 PRESTIGE & TRANSCEND
When you've grown as far as you can, perform a Rebirth. Trade your progress for powerful Nectar upgrades that make your next run even stronger. Master two layers of prestige and unlock the deepest secrets of the greenhouse.

🌸 ZERO FORCED ADS. EVER.
No pop-ups. No interruptions. No unskippable videos. Bloomlings respects your time. Choose to watch short ads for bonus Dewdrops — a special currency for exclusive cosmetics and rare Bloomlings. It's always your choice.

✨ FEATURES
• Satisfying tap gameplay with combos, crits, and idle production
• 28+ unique Bloomlings with lore, personalities, and evolution paths
• 8 biomes to explore, each with native species and bosses
• Deep upgrade system — Sunlight, Nectar, Essence, and Dewdrop shops
• Two prestige layers: Rebirth and Transcendence
• Synergy system — pair Bloomlings for powerful bonuses
• Daily rewards, achievements, and streak bonuses
• Plays offline — your Bloomlings keep growing while you're away
• No accounts required — your save stays on your device
• Dark theme UI designed for comfortable play
