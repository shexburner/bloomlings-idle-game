## Notes

- **Ads**: The app contains only voluntary rewarded video ads. No banner, interstitial, or forced ads. AdMob is configured as a mixed-audience app with child-safe ad content restrictions.
- **IAP**: None at launch. No real-money purchases of any kind.
- **UGC**: No user-generated content, no social features, no multiplayer.
- **Data**: No personal data collected by the app. AdMob collects device identifiers per its own privacy policy (disclosed in our privacy policy and data safety forms).
