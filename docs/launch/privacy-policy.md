Bloomlings ("the App", "we", "our", "us") is a mobile idle game developed by [Bloomlings Team / Company Name]. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

**Summary**: Bloomlings does not collect personal information. All game data is stored locally on your device. The only third-party service is Google AdMob, which serves optional rewarded ads.

---

## 1. Information Collection

**We do not collect any personal information.** The App requires no account, sign-up, email, name, or any other personally identifiable information (PII) to use.

We do not collect:
- Names, emails, or contact information
- Location data
- Photos, media, or files
- Contacts or address books
- Financial or payment information
- Usage analytics or behavioral data

## 2. Local Storage

The App stores game data (progress, currencies, Bloomling collection, settings) locally on your device using MMKV storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not backed up to any cloud service
- Can be deleted by uninstalling the App
- Can be manually exported/imported via local file operations

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to serve **voluntary rewarded video ads**. Ads are never forced — you choose when and whether to watch them.

When you interact with an ad, Google AdMob may collect:
- Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
- IP address (for geographic ad targeting)
- Device information (model, OS version)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We do not have access to this data.

- Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Ads Data Processing Terms: https://business.safety.google/adsprocessorterms/

### Local Notifications

The App uses local notifications (e.g., "Your Bloomlings miss you!") scheduled entirely on-device. No notification tokens or data are sent to any server.

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Bloomlings is rated E for Everyone / 4+ / PEGI 3. The App is a mixed-audience product and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

- The App collects **zero personal information** from any user, including children
- AdMob is configured as a mixed-audience app with appropriate ad content restrictions
- No behavioral tracking or profiling is performed

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through the App, please contact us at privacy@bloomlings.com. Since we collect no data, there is nothing to delete on our end — but we will assist you with any concerns.

## 5. GDPR Rights (European Economic Area)

For users in the EEA, the App presents a consent dialog on first launch (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ads are loaded. You may:

- **Consent** to personalized ads
- **Consent** to non-personalized ads only
- **Decline** all ads (no ads will be shown)

You may change your consent at any time in the App's settings.

Since the App itself stores no personal data on any server, the following GDPR rights apply only to data collected by Google AdMob:

- **Right of access / portability / erasure**: For ad-related data, submit requests through Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com/
- **Right to object**: Withdraw ad consent at any time via App settings
- **Local game data**: Delete by uninstalling the App

## 6. CCPA Rights (California)

For California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

- **We do not sell personal information.** We have never sold personal information.
- **Data collected**: The App itself collects no personal information. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad serving as described in Section 3.
- **Right to know / delete**: Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to disclose or delete on our end. For AdMob data, use Google's privacy tools.
- **Do Not Sell**: The App respects the `doNotSell` signal for ad personalization.

## 7. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5+, the App will request your permission before allowing AdMob to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, only non-personalized ads will be shown. The App functions identically regardless of your choice.

## 8. Data Security

- All game data is stored locally on your device
- Ad network communications use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
- No server-side data storage means no server-side breach risk
